# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to provide a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and respect boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, intimidation, sharing private information, and discriminatory conduct are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation. Report behavior concerns privately using GitHub's repository contact mechanisms; please do not post personal details in public issues.
